## Outline

- What a "web app" actually is: a browser, a URL, and something at the other end.
- **Static vs dynamic.** A static host only hands out files it already has. A
  dynamic host runs your code on every request. This one distinction decides the
  price, the deployment, and half the design.
- What you get for free, and what the free tier takes away: cold starts, sleep
  after inactivity, bandwidth caps, no persistent disk.
- Where your data lives when there is no database.
- The two routes this section covers, and when each is the right one:
  [Streamlit](streamlit/index.md) and [static web](static-web/index.md).
