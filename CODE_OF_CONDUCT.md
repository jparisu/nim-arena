# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in
NIM Arena a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful and welcoming to newcomers.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback (especially in PR review).
- Focusing on what is best for the project and community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Submitting malicious code or deliberately abusing the tournament/CI.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the
project maintainers via a private GitHub issue or direct message. Maintainers
will review and respond, and may remove content or contributors that violate
this code.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
